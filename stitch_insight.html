Spatial memory retention is significantly higher in 3D environments

Our research confirms that the human brain is biologically optimized for spatial awareness. Traditional 2D training modules—videos and text—require the user to "translate" flat information into physical actions, leading to rapid cognitive fatigue.

By leveraging spatial computing, we bypass this translation layer. Learning happens in-situ, where the muscle memory and visual context are fused. This "Obsidian Lens" approach results in 70% higher long-term retention compared to legacy learning systems.
